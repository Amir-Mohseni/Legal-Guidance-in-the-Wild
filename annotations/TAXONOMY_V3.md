# Legal-needs taxonomy v3

## Topics

- `FAMILY_AND_ESTATES` — Marriage, divorce, custody, support, adoption,
  guardianship, domestic violence, wills, trusts, probate, and inheritance.
- `HOUSING_AND_PROPERTY` — Rent, eviction, landlord–tenant matters, mortgages,
  foreclosure, ownership, conveyancing, zoning, land use, and building permits.
- `EMPLOYMENT_AND_LABOR` — Employment contracts, wages, dismissal,
  discrimination, harassment, leave, workplace safety, unions, and workers'
  compensation.
- `IMMIGRATION_AND_CITIZENSHIP` — Visas, work authorization, residence,
  asylum, citizenship, naturalization, deportation, and removal.
- `CRIMINAL_LAW` — Police, investigations, arrest, prosecution, criminal
  defense, sentencing, probation, victims' rights, criminal procedure, and
  criminal offenses.
- `TAX_LAW` — Personal and business taxation, including income, corporate,
  payroll, property, sales, VAT, and capital-gains tax; tax returns,
  deductions, audits, planning, compliance, and disputes with tax authorities.
- `CONSUMER_AND_PERSONAL_FINANCE` — Purchases, warranties, refunds, consumer
  scams, subscriptions, debt, debt collection, credit, loans, personal
  bankruptcy, banking, investments, and personal insurance.
- `BUSINESS_AND_COMMERCIAL` — Company formation and governance, partnerships,
  self-employment, commercial contracts, business compliance, and licensing.
- `TORTS_AND_CIVIL_LIABILITY` — Personal injury, negligence, malpractice,
  defamation, product liability, nuisance, wrongful death, and civil damages.
- `GOVERNMENT_AND_ADMINISTRATIVE` — Public benefits, pensions, welfare,
  professional licensing, permits, education rights, elections, and agency
  appeals.
- `CIVIL_RIGHTS_AND_CONSTITUTIONAL` — Discrimination, free speech, due process,
  equal protection, constitutional privacy, human rights, judicial review, and
  government powers.
- `DATA_PRIVACY_AND_TECHNOLOGY` — Data protection, privacy, consent, deletion,
  surveillance, platform practices and liability, data scraping, and AI
  regulation.
- `INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY` — Copyright, trademarks, patents, trade secrets,
  licensing, infringement, and ownership of creative or technical work.
- `OTHER` — Legal matters not covered by another topic.

## Request types

- `INFO_EXPLANATION` — Explanation of a legal concept or how the legal system
  works.
- `RIGHTS_OPTIONS_ADVICE` — Guidance about rights, obligations, options,
  liability, or what to do in a practical situation.
- `PROCEDURE_HOWTO` — Steps, forms, filings, deadlines, or where and how to
  lodge a claim, appeal, application, or complaint.
- `DOCUMENT_REVIEW` — Explanation, analysis, summary, or review of an existing
  legal document or case.
- `DOCUMENT_DRAFTING` — Drafting, writing, rewriting, or editing legal text.
- `OTHER_REQUEST` — A legal-content request not covered by another request
  type.

## User contexts

- `PERSONAL` — A real or planned situation faced by the user or someone they
  know.
- `PROFESSIONAL` — A business, organizational, practitioner, or operational
  setting.
- `ACADEMIC` — Study, coursework, teaching, or research.
- `GENERAL_CURIOSITY` — General or hypothetical interest without a practical
  or academic context.
- `CREATIVE_FICTION` — Fiction, roleplay, or worldbuilding.
