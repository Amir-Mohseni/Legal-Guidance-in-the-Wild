\section{Evaluation procedure}

The guidance models were calibrated on the silver validation split and then evaluated once on the human-reviewed gold evaluation set without further adjustment. Reliability intervals use 2,000 non-parametric conversation-level bootstrap resamples with replacement and random seed 42. Sampling is not stratified. Guidance and end-to-end intervals are percentile 95\% confidence intervals for macro-F1 over 200 resampled conversations. Topic macro-F1 uses the fixed set of 12 legal topics observed among the 84 guidance-positive gold conversations; end-to-end macro-F1 uses the 13 routing outcomes observed in the gold evaluation set.

Exact two-sided McNemar tests compare correctness on the same human-reviewed examples. Guidance and end-to-end comparisons use all 200 conversations, while topic-only comparisons use the 84 guidance-positive conversations.

\section{Paired significance tests}

\input{gold_significance.tex}

None of the eight comparisons reaches $p<.05$. These tests provide no statistically significant evidence that one evaluated system is superior to another. ModernBERT-large has the highest numerical full-conversation guidance macro-F1 (.845) and end-to-end macro-F1 (.593).

Human review changed the silver guidance decision for 8 of the 200 gold conversations. Across the six guidance detectors, accuracy on these cases ranges from .375 to .500, compared with .833 to .880 on the other 192. These cases may be more difficult, and the group is too small for a stable estimate. Among the 7 gold guidance-positive conversations whose silver primary topic changed, ModernBERT-base and ModernBERT-large each predict the final topic correctly in 5 cases, while TF--IDF does so in 3.
