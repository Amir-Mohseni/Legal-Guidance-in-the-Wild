\section{Evaluation procedure}

The guidance models were calibrated on the silver validation set and then evaluated once on the adjudicated gold set without further adjustment. Reliability intervals use 2,000 non-parametric conversation-level bootstrap resamples with replacement and random seed 42. Sampling is not stratified. Guidance and end-to-end intervals are percentile 95\% confidence intervals for macro-F1 over 200 resampled conversations. Topic macro-F1 uses the fixed set of 12 legal topics observed among the 84 guidance-positive gold conversations; end-to-end macro-F1 uses the 13 routing outcomes observed in the gold set.

Exact two-sided McNemar tests compare correctness on the same adjudicated examples. Guidance and end-to-end comparisons use all 200 conversations, while topic-only comparisons use the 84 guidance-positive conversations.

\section{Silver-label sensitivity}

The model-size ordering differs between the 290-conversation silver validation set and the 200-conversation human-adjudicated gold set. ModernBERT-base exceeds ModernBERT-large by 2.9 macro-F1 points on silver topic validation. On the gold topic task, ModernBERT-large scores .671 and ModernBERT-base .646, reversing the ordering by 2.5 points. ModernBERT-large also has the highest numerical full-conversation guidance macro-F1 (.845) and end-to-end macro-F1 (.593). The small gold set does not identify a single cause for the reversal.

Adjudication changed the silver guidance decision for 8 of the 200 gold conversations. Across the six guidance detectors, accuracy on these cases ranges from .375 to .500, compared with .833 to .880 on the other 192. These cases may reflect silver-label patterns learned by the models, but they are also likely more ambiguous, and the subgroup is too small for a stable estimate. Among the 7 gold guidance-positive conversations whose silver primary topic changed, ModernBERT-base and ModernBERT-large each predict the adjudicated topic correctly in 5 cases, while TF--IDF does so in 3.
